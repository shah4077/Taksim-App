Privacy Policy for Taksim

Last updated: 7 August 2026

In short: Taksim keeps your data on your phone. We have no servers, we run no analytics, we show no ads, and we never sell or share your information. The only time anything leaves your device is if you choose to create an email account, or if you tap a share button to send a summary through another app.

1. Who this policy covers

This policy explains how the Taksim mobile application ("Taksim", "the app") handles information. Taksim is a bill-splitting app for family gatherings and trips with friends. Contact: [YOUR CONTACT EMAIL].

2. Information stored on your device

Everything you enter into Taksim is saved locally on your phone, in the app's own private storage. It is not uploaded anywhere, and we cannot see it. This includes:

WhatExamples
Names you typeFamily names, friends' names, group members
Financial entriesContribution amounts, expenses, who paid, calculated balances
Event detailsGathering and trip names, dates, member counts
PreferencesLanguage (English/Arabic), currency selection

Because you may enter other people's names, please only add details you are comfortable recording. That information stays on your device just like your own.

3. Account information

Guest mode requires no account. Nothing is transmitted, and no personal details are requested.

If you instead sign up or sign in with an email address, your email and password are handled by Firebase Authentication, a service provided by Google. This is the only feature that sends personal information off your device. It is used solely to create and verify your login — never for marketing. Firebase's handling of that data is governed by Google's privacy policy.

Your gatherings, trips, and expense data remain on your device even with an email account. They are not synced or backed up to any server.

4. Sharing results with others

Taksim can prepare a plain-text summary of a split and hand it to WhatsApp or your email app when you tap a share button. The app only opens that other application with the message pre-filled — you decide whether to send it and to whom. Once handed over, the message is governed by the privacy policy of whichever app you chose.

5. What Taksim does not do

  • No advertising, and no advertising identifiers
  • No analytics, telemetry, crash tracking, or usage monitoring
  • No selling, renting, or sharing of personal information with third parties
  • No access to your contacts, camera, microphone, photos, or location
  • No tracking of you across other apps or websites

6. Retention and deleting your data

  • All app data: uninstalling Taksim permanently removes everything stored on the device. Deleted data cannot be recovered, as no copy exists elsewhere.
  • Individual records: gatherings, trips, families, friends, and expenses can be deleted inside the app at any time.
  • Your session: use Settings → Log out.
  • An email account: to delete the account itself, contact us at the address above and we will remove it from Firebase Authentication.

7. Children

Taksim is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. As the app stores data only on the device and collects nothing centrally, no such information is gathered in the ordinary course of use.

8. Security

App data is held in the private storage area that Android reserves for Taksim, which other apps cannot read. Protecting the device itself — with a screen lock, and by keeping the operating system updated — remains the best safeguard. Email authentication traffic is encrypted in transit by Firebase.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the revised version will be posted at this address with an updated date above. Material changes affecting how information is handled will also be noted in the app's store listing.
